Compiled code: does more itself instead of handing each operation back to the interpreter: `**`, physical values times or divided by a real, conversions of reals to integers, `deallocate`, string and array concatenations, copies, comparisons and fills of logic vectors of any length, `numeric_std` sums, differences and products, `is_x`, reductions and logical operators of vectors of up to 128 elements (also of vectors that hold metavalues such as `U`), the layout of a record or array whose bounds are known only at run time, slices whose length is known only at run time, and driving a record signal that changes only some of its fields or one element of an array of records. Designs with wide datapaths, record signals, many log messages or many small helper functions simulate faster.
