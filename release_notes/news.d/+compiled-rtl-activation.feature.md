Clocked designs of many small processes run faster: a process that waits again on the signal it waited on last no longer registers the wait (every tier), and processes compiled by `--tier=auto` and `--tier=llvm` enter faster (shared register scratch, array variables found once, short vector copies inline), about 10% on a design of thousands of one-assignment processes; `--tier=auto` now also compiles clocked processes of 140 instructions or more.
