`--cycle`, `--cycle-strict` and `--two-state` take a scope: `--cycle=PATH` and `--two-state=PATH` (repeatable; `tb.g` is every iteration of a generate, `tb.g(1)` one; vunit-yavs: the new sim option `yavs.cycle_scope`, and `yavs.two_state` takes a path or a list of paths). Without PATH `--two-state` (and `--cycle` next to it) now covers the design under test, which yavs finds from what the processes do and names on one line of standard error: the testbench and its verification components keep the nine values, so bus models that check a line for `'Z'` work under `--two-state`. Before, `--two-state` covered everything below the top; give the top-level unit's name as PATH for the whole design. Plain `--cycle` still takes the whole design. `--cycle-report` now modifies `--cycle` or `--cycle-strict` and is an error alone.
