Cycle mode: `--wave` and `--surfer` now work with `--cycle` and `--cycle-strict` (in a VUnit run, `--yavs-surfer` with `yavs.cycle`). Signals inside the region are recorded per time step from the region's own values, and `--wave-include` and `--wave-exclude` choose them too, so a narrow selection costs little; the cycle mode page lists what differs from a normal-mode waveform.
