A large design keeps the whole of its heap on transparent huge pages (Linux with glibc, THP in `madvise` mode), not only the first 64 MiB, which cuts its data-TLB misses; nothing has to be set. Setting the glibc allocator tunables `top_pad`, `trim_threshold`, `mmap_threshold` or `hugetlb` (or their older `MALLOC_*_` variables) now leaves the allocator to you, and `YAVS_HUGE_HEAP=0` still switches it off.
