Vector operations: operations on vectors work a word at a time instead of element by element, so wide values simulate faster: the ordering operators on one-dimensional arrays, `/=` of logic vectors, the `numeric_std` matching relations and the relations of `unsigned` and `signed` on vectors of any length, the `std_logic_1164` logical operators between a vector and a single `std_ulogic`, matching `case?` statements, `to_string` of vectors, and `write` of a logic vector to a textio line. For `bit_vector` and `boolean_vector` this holds for shifts, rotations, reductions, logical operators with a scalar operand, `?=`, `?/=`, `to_hstring`, `to_ostring`, `minimum` and `maximum`.
