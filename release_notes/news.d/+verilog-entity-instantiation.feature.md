A Verilog module can be instantiated from VHDL as `entity lib.module`, without a component declaration: its parameters are the generics, its ports `std_logic` and `std_logic_vector` (sized by the generic map where the module's range is an expression of parameters, also with `$clog2`, shifts and `?:`), the library named is searched first and then the `-L` libraries, and errors in the maps name the module and show the Verilog declaration beside the VHDL one.
