A Verilog net can have several drivers: `wire` and `tri` nets resolve them as the standard describes (three-state buses with `assign y = en ? a : 1'bz;`, drivers of different bits), `tri0`, `tri1`, `supply0`, `supply1` and `uwire` nets work, and a drive strength on a continuous assignment is honoured on three levels (strong, weak, high impedance). VHDL sees the resolved value, with `L` and `H` for weak levels.
