vunit-yavs compiles in the project's dependency order with more files per `yavs analyze` call: the files of one library, standard and flags that are ready together share a call, not only consecutive ones. `-j N` (`--jobs N`) runs up to N calls at once, into different libraries; without it, one call at a time. Failures, skipped dependents, `--keep-compiling` and the output stay those of VUnit's own compile loop.
