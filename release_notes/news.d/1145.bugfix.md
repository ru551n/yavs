A REAL signal, or an element of a `real_vector` or of any composite with real fields, going from `0.0` to `-0.0` (or back) is no longer an event: it is still a transaction (`'active`, `'transaction`), but no process waits on it, `'event` stays false, and `'last_value` and the waveform do not change, as `=` already treats the two zeros as equal. Other signal types are unchanged.
