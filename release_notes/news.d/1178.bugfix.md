A run-time range or index error now shows its values and bounds the way VHDL writes them: an enumeration value as its literal (`value WHITE outside of RGB range RED to BLUE`, a character literal quoted), a physical value with its unit (`value 20 NS outside of SHORT range 1 NS to 10 NS`, in the largest unit that divides it exactly), and the index of an array indexed by an enumeration or character the same way. Before, enumeration values showed as positions and physical values as a bare count of the smallest unit. Floating-point values show in full and a descending subtype shows `downto`, as before.
