A value driven through a scalar `out` or `inout` signal parameter that does not belong to the subtype of the actual signal, such as `o <= 10` in a procedure called with a signal of `integer range 0 to 9`, is now a run-time range error at the assignment. Before, the signal took the value. Calls whose actual holds every value of the formal's subtype are unchanged and pay nothing; a procedure that declares its own subprograms is not checked yet.
