A value passing through a port that does not belong to the subtype receiving it, such as an `out integer` port driving 10 into a signal of `integer range 0 to 9` or an `in natural` port reading -1 from an `integer` signal, is now a run-time range error naming the port. Conversion results in port maps are checked the same way. Designs whose values all fit keep running, and ports whose subtypes match pay nothing.
