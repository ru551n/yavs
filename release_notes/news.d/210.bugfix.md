A subprogram call may associate a formal by its sub-elements, such as `pr(x(0) => s)` for an unconstrained `out` signal formal or `p(r.i => 1, r.b => '0')` for a record: the call now resolves at analysis instead of reporting `E1301`. A non-static index or range in a formal sub-element name, in calls and port maps, is rejected with `E1410` instead of being taken as the whole formal.
