Processes resumed in the same simulation cycle now run in a fixed order chosen by their shape:
processes with a sensitivity list or a single wait statement first, in elaboration order, then
the other processes in reverse elaboration order, those woken by a signal before those woken by a
timeout. Testbenches that check, in the same cycle, a value reported by a process of an
instantiated entity now behave as they do in other simulators.
