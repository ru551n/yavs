Compiled clocked RTL (`--tier=llvm`, `--tier=auto`) answers `rising_edge`, `falling_edge` and `'event`, reads signals of every element kind and length, drives vectors of any length and signals of enumeration, integer, boolean or real elements, compares logic vectors and keeps the layouts of records in the process's frame without leaving the compiled code, and its rarely taken paths are out of line; a process that waits on several signals no longer names them again at every activation, in the interpreter too. `YAVS_TIER_CENSUS=1` prints what compiled code still sends to the host.
