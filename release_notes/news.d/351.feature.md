Coverage exclusions: `-- coverage off` / `-- coverage on` / `-- coverage off-line` comments leave code out of the coverage database, `yavs cov report --uncovered` prints every miss as a row you can paste into an exclusion file, and `yavs cov report --exclude FILE` leaves those items out of the totals and Cobertura report, listing them with their reasons. Rows match by the statement's text, so edits that move a statement do not break them; stale rows, moved lines and excluded items that ran are warnings.
