Breakpoint conditions, Watch expressions and `examine` can call functions of the design and of packages such as `numeric_std` (`to_integer(unsigned(d)) > 40`), convert types, use the operators a type declares (`cnt + 1 = 4` on an `unsigned`) and read the attributes `'event`, `'active`, `'last_value`, `'last_event` and `'last_active` of signals and `'length`, `'left`, `'right`, `'high`, `'low` and `'ascending` of arrays. Impure functions are called too when they only read: signals of their architecture, `now`, and functions without parameters by their bare name (`edges > 2`). A called function runs apart from the simulation on copies of what it reads, so a condition cannot change it; a function that may write (a signal, a shared or protected variable, a file, `std.env.stop`) is refused, naming what it does and where.
