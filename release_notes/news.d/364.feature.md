Breakpoint conditions, Watch expressions and `examine` can call pure functions of the design and of packages such as `numeric_std` (`to_integer(unsigned(d)) > 40`), convert types, use the operators a type declares (`cnt + 1 = 4` on an `unsigned`) and read the attributes `'event`, `'active`, `'last_value`, `'last_event` and `'last_active` of signals and `'length`, `'left`, `'right`, `'high`, `'low` and `'ascending` of arrays. A called function runs apart from the simulation, so a condition cannot change it; impure functions are refused with the reason.
