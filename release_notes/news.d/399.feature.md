`yavs-gui` can debug: a source view with a breakpoint gutter (click to toggle, right click for a condition and hit count), the current line highlighted at a stop, a call stack for every process, locals and signals with lazily expanded records and paged arrays (double click to change a local), watch expressions, hover values, step over, in and out, step to the next delta or time step, run for a time, and the time and delta of the stop in the status bar.
