Testbenches with several clocks that edge at the same times (clocks of equal or related periods, timeouts that fall on clock edges) simulate faster: those time steps no longer take the kernel's general update path.
The idle skip of clocked processes also works in runs with several threads: they skip the same activations as a single-threaded run.
Designs with large processes (a CPU core, wide pipelines) simulate up to 5% faster: the processes keep their registers between activations instead of copying a template at every one.
