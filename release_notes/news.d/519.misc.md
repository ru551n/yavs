Wide buses passed through ports and the processes that wait on them need less kernel work per delta cycle: activity of wide signals is recorded per block of elements, and an event with many changed runs no longer walks the lists of each run when every process waits for the whole signal. The reduction operators of `std_logic_1164` and `numeric_std` and the `*_reduce` functions of `std_logic_misc` run natively on whole words of a vector.
