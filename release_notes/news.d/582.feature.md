A simulation that is stuck or hung now says why: the delta cycle limit error names the processes and signals of the loop and how to raise the limit (`--max-deltas`), `--hang-report` (and `kill -USR1` on a running simulation, on Unix) lists what every waiting process waits for, which signals have no driver and which clock stopped, a process with no wait statement is warned about before time 0, and a shuffled run prints its seed and how to replay it.
