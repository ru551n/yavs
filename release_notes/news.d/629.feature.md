yavs now analyzes and simulates VHDL-93 code: `--std=93` (or `1993`) selects the 1993 language and the 1993 `std` and `ieee` packages, where `std_logic_vector` is a type of its own, shared variables can have any type, and names that 2008 reserved or predefined are free. A design of 93 files runs against the 1993 packages; a 93 file in a 2008 design is checked as 93.
