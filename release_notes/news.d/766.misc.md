Compiled clocked processes (`--tier=auto`, `--tier=llvm`) hand their zero-delay signal assignments and their waits to the simulation kernel directly, and a compiled `wait until rising_edge(clk)` process now costs the same as the sensitivity-list forms of a clocked process. Every activation of a compiled process also enters its compiled code with less overhead. A compiled clocked process that calls no subprogram and uses no access or shared objects starts each activation with less bookkeeping still.
